## 0. Vision and Product Pillars (owner direction 2026-11-15)

PunyaSitus helps Indonesian businesses "naik kelas" in digital marketing: more visibility, traffic and conversions. It serves owners without a website and owners already on WordPress who want everything simpler. Every feature is judged by one question: does it help the client become better known or convert more?

| # | Pillar | Meaning |

## 1. Overview

**PunyaSitus** is a cloud-native, multi-tenant Content Management System (CMS) engineered from the ground up for the Indonesian business landscape. It replaces the legacy WordPress + Elementor paradigm with a centrally hosted, HTML-first platform behind Cloudflare. 

Instead of requiring individual hosting accounts, complex PHP updates, and vulnerable third-party plugins, PunyaSitus runs as a centralized multi-tenant platform where hundreds of businesses operate on custom domains or platform subdomains with automated SSL and sub-second page loads.

PunyaSitus delivers the administrative clarity of WordPress—hierarchical menus, static pages, blog articles, media management, and automated on-page SEO—while replacing fragile shopping carts with a purpose-built **Mini-Catalog** featuring **WhatsApp (WA) Click-to-Chat CTA** and **External Link Out** capabilities. The system incorporates an out-of-the-box **Default Modular Theme** with an intuitive visual section customizer, alongside an isolated **Custom Theme Engine** delivered as an agency service tier for high-end clients across Travel/Umrah, Corporate Brands, Offline Outlets/Gerai, and Professional Services.

## 2. Product, Audience, and Market Context

### 2.1 The Indonesian Market Reality

Assumption: the following audience hypotheses motivate the product. No full market study was supplied with this pack (a desk check of official statistics and competitors is in section 16 and BUILD-LOG 2026-11-14); validate them in early customer interviews before using them in commercial claims.

1. **Maintenance overhead**: Some target businesses may benefit from centrally managed software and fewer third-party plugins. Adoption, compromise rates, and existing site performance are unverified.
2. **Conversational commerce**: WhatsApp inquiries and marketplace links are the selected conversion flows. Their effectiveness relative to checkout must be measured for the chosen verticals; no conversion percentage is established.
3. **Agency efficiency**: Central updates may reduce per-site maintenance while increasing the impact of platform regressions. Post-launch measurements must include support time and release failures.
4. **B2B Compro, Personal Branding & WhatsApp-Closing Verticals (`DEC-038`)**:
   - **Corporate & Brand Profiles (`compro`)**: B2B companies, contractors, manufacturers, clinics, schools, and institutions presenting value propositions, credentials (`NIB`/licences), portfolio/case studies, and B2B inquiries.
   - **Sales Compro & Personal Branding (`sales`)**: Sales executives (automotive, property, insurance), official agents, independent consultants, doctors, lawyers, speakers, and professionals building personal/specialist credibility and direct consultation leads.
   - **Travel & Umrah Agencies (`travel` — Agency Showcase, NOT an OTA/Ticketing Portal)**: Tour & Umrah bureau profiles showcasing multi-day itineraries, package inclusions, and departure schedules that convert via WhatsApp/inquiry consultation — **never** a multi-vendor OTA portal, flight/hotel ticketing engine, or live seat-booking system.
   - **Offline Compro & Multi-Branch Outlets (`resto` / `branches`)**: Physical businesses (outlets, F&B, clinics, workshops, showrooms) highlighting branch directories, operating hours, Google Maps directions, and walk-in / WhatsApp inquiries.
   - **Showcase Catalog & Dealerships (`catalog` / `dealer`)**: Site owners adapt their offerings (products, wholesale items, cars/motorcycles with static credit estimates) to close directly via **WhatsApp (`wa.me`)**, inquiry forms, or external marketplace links.

## 3. Goals

- **G-1: Public site Performance**: Target p75 mobile LCP < 1.5s, CLS < 0.05, and INP < 150ms for the supported default theme, deliberately stricter than Google's "good" Core Web Vitals thresholds (LCP <= 2.5s, INP <= 200ms, CLS <= 0.1 at p75; web.dev/articles/vitals, retrieved 2026-11-14). These are product targets, not guarantees for every visitor or third-party script. REQ-040 defines lab acceptance and field measurement.
- **G-2: Secure Multi-Tenant Architecture**: Enable centralized platform updates where all tenant websites run on one shared Cloudflare Worker with strict `tenant_id` database isolation and automated custom domain SSL provisioning.
- **G-3: High-Converting WhatsApp & Link-Out Mini Catalog**: Provide a lightweight product/package catalog allowing visitors to initiate structured WhatsApp chats with pre-filled inquiries or navigate to external marketplaces without cart friction.
- **G-4: Effortless Native CMS Management**: Deliver a clean, non-technical admin interface to manage hierarchical menus, static pages, blog articles, hero banners, and media assets with zero coding required.
- **G-5: Automated SEO & Social Sharing Engine**: Automatically emit Schema.org structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, TravelAgency, Product, Article, BreadcrumbList) and pristine Open Graph tags tailored specifically for WhatsApp chat link previews (at most 300 KiB).
- **G-6: Dual Theme Architecture**: Support both a zero-code Default Modular Theme (visual customizer) and an isolated Custom Theme Engine for agency-tier client services.

### Business model

Custom theme development is the core paid service: brands commission a bespoke theme built by the platform team (REQ-063, docs/themes/THEME-DEVELOPMENT.md). The subscription (IDR 179,000 per website per calendar month, or IDR 1,790,000 per year, plus the payment channel fee; one price covers every offering type, DEC-035 C1/C2) covers hosting, maintenance and the CMS; the default theme serves self-service clients and trials. Delivering and maintaining custom themes quickly and safely is therefore a first-class product goal, not an add-on (DEC-015).

## 4. Non-Goals

- **NG-1: No Arbitrary User-Uploaded / Third-Party Code Plugins** (clarified by `DEC-037`, `ADR-0010`): The platform will NOT support user-uploaded `.zip`/PHP/JS plugins or unreviewed third-party code execution. Extensibility is provided strictly through code-reviewed first-party themes and modular plugins (`REQ-096..099`).
- **NG-2: Not an E-Commerce Store, OTA Portal, or Ticketing Engine** (`DEC-008`, `DEC-038`): PunyaSitus is a B2B company-profile, personal-branding, agency-showcase, and catalog-to-WhatsApp CMS — **not** an e-commerce store, online travel aggregator (OTA), or flight/hotel/event ticketing system. There is no cart, visitor checkout or payment, order ledger, live seat/stock inventory, shipping calculator, visitor coupon, or visitor account (platform subscription/add-on coupons under REQ-082 are tenant-to-platform billing only). Visitor conversions route strictly via WhatsApp (`wa.me`), inquiry forms, or external links.
- **NG-3: No End-Consumer Accounts**: Public site visitors do not register accounts. Tenant members, agency users, affiliates and platform operators are the only authenticated users.
- **NG-4: No Unconstrained Drag-and-Drop Page Builder**: Avoid dirty HTML/CSS DOM trees. Customization is strictly section-based and modular.
- **NG-5: Single Primary WhatsApp in Base Core** (narrowed by `DEC-037`, `ADR-0010`): Base CMS settings and catalog items keep one designated WhatsApp number so default onboarding stays simple; optional multi-agent WhatsApp CS lead rotation is delivered cleanly as an opt-in first-party plugin (`wa-cs-rotator`, `REQ-099`) without complicating base CMS screens.
- **NG-6: No Raw Custom Script Injection**: CMS inputs accept only verified structured analytics IDs (Meta Pixel, GA4, TikTok Pixel and one Google Tag Manager container ID); there is no raw HTML/JS header/footer field. Enabling a tenant-owned GTM container (REQ-027, DEC-027) knowingly delegates script trust on that tenant's public pages to the container's publishers; admin and other tenants stay isolated. Raw HTML/JS injection is disallowed for security and CSP integrity.
- **NG-7: No General Redirect Engine or 1-Click Export Tooling** (narrowed by DEC-016): regex/wildcard, external-target and cross-domain redirect rules and bulk JSON/XML export tools are out of scope for v1 (the owner-only leads CSV export under REQ-069 is the single bounded exception). Explicitly in scope: the automatic former-slug 308 for the same resource (REQ-061) and custom-domain alias redirects (REQ-043). Bounded same-host migration redirects (REQ-067) are release 1.1 (DEC-035 C16). The owner's full-website export archive (REQ-107, DEC-043) is a second bounded exception.

## 5. Requirements (EARS Syntax)

### 5.1 Multi-Tenancy & Host-Based Domain Routing

- **REQ-001** (Ubiquitous; amended 2026-11-18, DEC-036): The system shall isolate all tenant website content, leads and analytics inside a dedicated per-tenant SQLite Durable Object (`TenantStore`), prefix all R2 objects under `t/{tenantId}/`, and scope all shared `PLATFORM_DB` control-plane records by an indexed `tenant_id`.
- **REQ-002** (Event-driven): When an HTTP request reaches the application, the system shall resolve the active tenant context using the incoming `Host` header (matching platform subdomain under the configured base domain (`*.situsku.click`, DEC-045) or verified custom domain).
- **REQ-003** (Unwanted): If the incoming hostname does not resolve to an active tenant, then the system shall return an HTTP 404 tenant-not-found page without exposing internal database errors.
- **REQ-004** (Event-driven): When a tenant owner adds a custom domain, the system shall verify CNAME/TXT records and automatically initiate SSL provisioning via Cloudflare for SaaS.
- **REQ-005** (State-driven; amended 2026-11-18, DEC-035 C5): While a website is `suspended` (from `H+8` after paid expiry, upon unpaid trial expiry, or under an operator hold), the system shall return a public `503` maintenance page (*"Situs dalam perbaikan"*) with `no-store` and `noindex`, and restrict dashboard access to the payment/recovery shell (including the `REQ-069` owner leads CSV export, sign-out and tenant/agency switching; non-payers see a payer-contact notice). During `locked` (`H+4..H+7`), the public site remains live (`200`) and indexable while CMS writes are blocked (`REQ-060`). Payment never automatically lifts an operator hold.

### 5.2 WordPress-Equivalent Core CMS

- **REQ-006** (Ubiquitous): The system shall provide a hierarchical Navigation Menu Builder supporting multi-level navigation trees (Header Primary Menu, Mobile Drawer Menu, Footer Quick Links).
- **REQ-007** (Event-driven): When an admin saves a menu structure, the system shall validate that no circular parent-child references exist and serialize the tree as an ordered JSON hierarchy.
- **REQ-008** (Ubiquitous; amended 2026-11-18, DEC-038): The system shall provide a Page Manager supporting modular pages (About Us, Services, Sales Landing Pages, Privacy, Terms) with customizable URL slugs, status (`draft`, `published`), SEO overrides, and a **Landing Page Focus Mode** toggle (`standard` vs `focused` hiding top/footer navigation menus so ad-campaign and personal-branding squeeze pages focus 100% on the offer and WhatsApp CTA).
- **REQ-009** (Ubiquitous): The system shall provide a Blog Article Manager with rich-text editing, featured image, categories, tags, author attribution, publication timestamp, and reading time estimation.
- **REQ-010** (Event-driven; amended 2026-11-18, DEC-035 C8 & DEC-036): When an image is uploaded to the Media Library, the system shall accept files up to 5 MB (5,000,000 bytes) decided by content in JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF, GIF (first frame) or TIFF (first page), plus AVIF converted client-side to PNG/WebP before upload (since Cloudflare Images AVIF input is Enterprise-only; SVG and BMP are rejected), normalize on the server via the Cloudflare Images binding to at most 2,000,000 bytes and 2560 px on the longest edge stripped of EXIF/location metadata, publish only WebP responsive derivatives (including a byte-budgeted hero variant), and require descriptive ALT text unless explicitly decorative. Acceptance: a valid 5,000,000-byte source passes and 5,000,001 bytes fails server-side; iPhone HEIC converts cleanly; BMP, SVG and spoofed/corrupt inputs fail safely; public derivatives decode as WebP with image/webp MIME; private originals are never public output; quota counts normalized bytes (REQ-064).
- **REQ-011** (Ubiquitous): The system shall provide a Site Identity settings panel for site title, tagline, logo (light & dark mode), favicon, and primary brand contact information.

### 5.3 Mini-Catalog & Conversational CTA Engine

- **REQ-012** (Ubiquitous; amended 2026-11-18, DEC-035 C3): The system shall provide typed offerings: `service` at /layanan/{slug}, `product` at /produk/{slug}, `tour` at /tour/{slug} and `vehicle` at /kendaraan/{slug} (REQ-079), stored in one offerings model with real columns plus validated type attributes (CMSPRO ADR-0003), organized by tenant categories, each with a cover image and an ordered gallery of up to 12 images, and a website may enable several types at no extra cost.
- **REQ-013** (State-driven; amended 2026-11-18): While configuring an offering, the editor shall select the call to action: `whatsapp` (chat with a prefilled message), `external_link` (redirect to a safe HTTPS URL), `inquiry` (built-in or tenant form, REQ-080) or `book` (inquiry form flagged as a booking request with no reservation, stock or schedule lock) (CMSPRO DEC-044).
- **REQ-014** (State-driven): While Action Type is `whatsapp`, the system shall format the click-to-chat URL with the tenant's designated WhatsApp number (validated Indonesian E.164 format `628xxxxxxxxxx`) and a customizable dynamic message template containing `{{item_title}}`, `{{item_price}}`, and `{{page_url}}`.
- **REQ-015** (State-driven): While Action Type is `external_link`, the system shall validate that the target URL is a safe HTTPS link (Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop, Booking Engine, Google Form) and render it with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
- **REQ-016** (Ubiquitous; amended 2026-11-18): The system shall display offering prices with Indonesian Rupiah formatting (`Rp 1.500.000`) using the price models `fixed`, `from`, `range` (with a maximum not below the price) and `on_request`, an optional negotiable flag, optional strikethrough original price and highlight badges ("Promo", "Best Seller", "Slot Terbatas", "Habis"); an on-request price never reads as free.
- **REQ-017** (Event-driven): When a visitor clicks a WhatsApp CTA or External Link, the system shall emit a lightweight, non-blocking click-event signal to the tenant analytics counter and trigger configured client-side pixel events (`Lead` or `Contact`).

### 5.4 Theme System & Visual Customizer

Site-wide widgets are configured separately: header menu (two levels), footer, announcement banner, floating WhatsApp button with tenant-configured availability text (never an inferred live-presence badge) and the consent banner (CMSPRO ADR-0009, DEC-020).

### 5.5 Comprehensive SEO & Social Previews

- **REQ-022** (Ubiquitous): The system shall automatically inject Schema.org JSON-LD structured data on all public site pages:
  - `Organization` or `LocalBusiness` / `TravelAgency` on Homepage
  - `Product` with `Offer` only for genuine products with visible, valid price and availability; service/travel pages use appropriate factual schema without invented offers
  - `Article` with `author`, `datePublished`, and `image` on Blog pages
  - `FAQPage` on pages containing visible FAQ sections (semantic markup only; Google no longer shows the FAQ rich result as of 2026-05-07)
  - `BreadcrumbList` on all hierarchical child pages
- **REQ-023** (Ubiquitous): The system shall generate dynamic Open Graph (OG) and Twitter Card meta tags for every page, ensuring crisp 1200x630px social cards and thumbnails specifically formatted for WhatsApp chat previews (compressed to at most 300 KiB = 307,200 bytes, matching PLAN section 7.3).
- **REQ-024** (Ubiquitous): The system shall serve a dynamic, tenant-scoped `/sitemap.xml` containing only published, canonical, indexable pages, articles, offerings and eligible archives, with accurate `lastmod` timestamps under PLAN.md section 15.
- **REQ-025** (Ubiquitous): The system shall serve a dynamic, tenant-scoped `/robots.txt` referencing the tenant's canonical sitemap URL.
- **REQ-026** (State-driven): While rendering a public content page eligible for a canonical, the system shall include one absolute `<link rel="canonical">` matching the verified primary origin and normalized route; private, error and redirect responses follow PLAN.md section 15 instead of inventing a canonical destination.
- **REQ-027** (State-driven; amended by DEC-027): While rendering public site pages, the system shall load either validated direct GA4, Meta and TikTok identifiers or one tenant-owned Google Tag Manager container (never both for the same vendor) only after applicable consent, and shall make GTM setup easy: a validated container ID field, a standard first-party dataLayer event contract, a downloadable ready-to-import container template and an Indonesian step-by-step guide. The tenant owns what its container runs; the platform shows that responsibility before enabling it and keeps an audited operator kill switch. PLAN.md section 18.1 defines the contract.

### 5.6 Native Lead Capture & Analytics Signal Events

- **REQ-031** (Event-driven; amended DEC-042): When a visitor submits the contact/lead inquiry form, the system shall validate the inputs (Name, Phone/WA, Email, Message), persist the record to the `leads` table with status `new`, display a success state, and send the owner one PII-free email notice per lead ("Ada pesan masuk baru" with a link to the inbox, never the visitor's message or contact data) through `EmailPort`, which owners can turn off. - **REQ-032** (Ubiquitous): The system shall provide a Leads Management Inbox in the tenant admin dashboard allowing owners and admins to view incoming inquiries, filter by status (`new`, `contacted`, `closed`), and 1-click open WhatsApp chat with the lead.
- **REQ-033** (Event-driven): When a visitor clicks a WhatsApp CTA or successfully submits a Lead Form, the public site shall dispatch client-side conversion events (`Lead` / `Contact`) to the active Meta, TikTok, and Google analytics pixels.

## 6. Stack & Constraints

- **Architecture Profile**: 100% Cloudflare Multi-Tenant Monorepo (`apps/web` incl. Worker fetch, Queue consumer, Cron `scheduled`, `TenantStore` SQLite Durable Object, and `VideoTranscoder` Container; `packages/db`, `packages/auth`, `packages/core`) aligned with `adsbook.id` per ADR-0007, ADR-0009 and DEC-036.
- **Application Runtime** (ADR-0009, accepted 2026-11-18): One Astro SSR application on `@astrojs/cloudflare` (`output: "server"`, `session: false`, `nodejs_compat`) + Tailwind CSS v4, deployed as one Cloudflare Worker (`punyasitus-web`). It serves the platform marketing site (`punyasitus.com`), tenant/agency/affiliate app (`app.punyasitus.com`), superadmin console (`console.punyasitus.com`), and every tenant subdomain (`*.situsku.click`) or custom domain via Cloudflare for SaaS fallback origin. Background work runs on Cloudflare Queues, Cron Triggers, and per-tenant Durable Object `alarm()`. No VPS, Coolify, or external database server (the tiny stateless static-IP relay for AutoLaris H2H is the sole external network hop).
- **Admin & Portal Surfaces**: Server-rendered Astro pages on `app.punyasitus.com` and `console.punyasitus.com` with React 19 islands, Tailwind CSS v4, TanStack Table, and **`shadcn/ui` preset `base-nova`** (`@base-ui/react` primitives exclusively, `rsc: false`, Lucide icons; never Radix UI). Admin CSS and islands never load on tenant public hosts.
- **Language and UI Contract**: Strict TypeScript (`noUncheckedIndexedAccess`) across Worker, Durable Objects, Containers and shared packages. `DESIGN.md` owns tokens, `base-nova` component rules, responsive desktop + mobile web-app shells, and rendered acceptance.
- **Database & Persistence** (DEC-036, no Supabase/external PostgreSQL): Two-tier Cloudflare SQLite with Drizzle ORM:
  1. **`PLATFORM_DB` (Cloudflare D1, `apac`, 10 GB)** for authoritative cross-tenant control plane (Better Auth users/sessions/Google OAuth/2FA/MCP OAuth, `tenants` & `tenant_hosts` routing table, billing obligations/attempts/settlements/numbered receipts, coupons, agencies, affiliates & payouts, marketing funnel, service packages/requests, abuse queue, platform audit).
  2. **`TenantStore` (SQLite-backed Durable Object per tenant, 10 GB per website, `locationHint: "apac-se"`)** for physically isolated tenant website data (pages & typed sections, blog posts & revisions, 4-type offerings & categories, menus, media metadata & quota ledger, forms & leads inbox, branches, business profile, cookieless analytics, activity log), accessed through a `D1Database`-compatible RPC adapter with synchronous `transactionSync` batches and 30-day PITR bookmarks.
- **Authentication**: Per-request Better Auth (`better-auth` with `drizzleAdapter(..., { provider: "sqlite" })`, `advanced.database.joins: true`), "Masuk dengan Google" primary + email/password fallback, Organization plugin, `twoFactor` TOTP (mandatory for superadmin and agency admins), `mcp` OAuth 2.1 provider, and `__Host-` session cookies.
- **Object Storage & Media**: Cloudflare R2 (`t/<tenantId>/...`) for originals, WebP derivatives, videos and immutable PDF receipts; Cloudflare Images binding for responsive WebP normalization; scale-to-zero Cloudflare Containers (`ffmpeg`) for non-H.264 MOV/WebM video transcoding.
- **CDN, Edge & Domains**: Dedicated SaaS zone (`situsku.click`) with Cloudflare for SaaS Custom Hostnames API, HTTP DCV pass-through, O2O support, Turnstile bot protection, and managed WAF/AI-crawler rules.

## 7. Technical Decisions (ADR-Lite)

- **DEC-001: Multi-Tenant Hostname Resolution**
  - **Decision**: The historical KV-first design is subject to ADR-0006's correction: Cloudflare routes every hostname to the app Worker (ADR-0009), and application middleware resolves verified hostname ownership and tenant status authoritatively before serving public content.
  - **Why**: Cached public content can reduce rendering work. Host ownership and tenant status require authoritative validation; see ADR-0006, accepted for this item by DEC-029.
  - **Consequence**: No public HTML cache may bypass tenant revocation checks. Static assets remain CDN-cacheable; domain changes need reconciliation with Cloudflare.

- **DEC-002: Built-in Native Features over Plugin Architecture**
  - **Decision**: Reject WordPress-style plugin marketplace in favor of native, configurable feature sets (SEO, WhatsApp engine, theme customizer, analytics).
  - **Why**: A controlled feature set reduces the integration surface. Platform security maintenance and outages remain possible; no vulnerability percentage or uptime guarantee is established.
  - **Consequence**: Advanced custom logic must be built by platform maintainers or delivered via custom theme hooks.

- **DEC-003: WhatsApp CTA & Link-Out over Internal Checkout Cart**
  - **Decision**: Focus catalog conversion on direct WhatsApp chat dispatch and external marketplace link-outs rather than an internal shopping cart.
  - **Why**: Indonesian consumer behavior for company profiles, tour bookings, and SME services relies heavily on WhatsApp negotiations; building payment custody, courier APIs, and cart checkout in v1 introduces high abandonment and massive development complexity.
  - **Consequence**: Order tracking is managed via WhatsApp CRM conversations and external conversion signals rather than a centralized checkout order ledger.

- **DEC-004: Modular Section Registry for Default Theme**
  - **Decision**: Construct the Default Theme from a finite set of pre-built, battle-tested section components configured via a clean JSON state in the Admin rather than free-form drag-and-drop.
  - **Why**: Protects design integrity, prevents broken mobile layouts, and ensures clean semantic HTML for SEO and sub-second Core Web Vitals.
  - **Consequence**: There is no custom HTML section, raw script field, or tenant-provided CSS. Rich text uses a validated, sanitized document schema.

## 9. Engineering and Delivery Requirements

The roadmap dates are estimates, not commitments. Complete dependency gates in TASKS.md before scheduling a release (DEC-030: full development, single production release). Each requirement below adds missing behavior necessary to deliver the existing product; it does not introduce visitor checkout or a public anonymous content API. AI writing help (REQ-075) and the authenticated tenant MCP server (REQ-076) were added later by DEC-026 and ADR-0008. Platform subscription payments are explicitly added by REQ-056 through REQ-058.

- **REQ-034** (Ubiquitous): The system shall authenticate members, verify organization membership on every tenant operation, enforce owner/admin/editor permissions server-side, and revoke access after membership removal. Acceptance: role and cross-tenant read/write tests pass, including direct API requests and stale sessions.
- **REQ-035** (Event-driven): When an operator provisions a tenant or changes its service state, the system shall atomically create required defaults or audit the state transition; invitations shall expire, be single-use, and bind to the invited identity. Acceptance: retry cannot duplicate a tenant, and an editor cannot invite members or change service state.
- **REQ-036** (Event-driven): When content or theme settings are saved or published, the system shall validate versioned input, keep draft edits private, reject stale writes with 409, and publish an atomic snapshot. Acceptance: preview is authorized and uncacheable; public page, sitemap, and metadata observe the same published revision.
- **REQ-037** (Ubiquitous): The admin shall provide usable screens for every CMS module, theme configuration, domains, members, leads, and service status, with explicit loading, empty, validation, saving, failure, and permission states. Acceptance: browser journeys create, preview, publish, edit, and unpublish content on mobile and desktop with keyboard access.
- **REQ-038** (Unwanted): If an anonymous form or event request exceeds its input, rate, replay, or tenant boundary, the system shall reject it without persistence; lead retries shall not create duplicates. Acceptance: forged tenant selectors, oversized bodies, repeated submissions, and storage failures exercise the documented error states.
- **REQ-039** (Ubiquitous): The system shall minimize personal data, gate optional tracking and embeds by consent, redact logs, enforce bounded retention, and support an operator-assisted access/deletion process. Acceptance: no tracking before consent, no form PII in analytics/logs, and deletion/retention tests pass including restore handling. Breach notices and data-subject requests follow the 3 x 24-hour duties of UU 27/2022 with the roles in DEC-025 (tenant controls visitor leads, PunyaSitus processes them). Legal applicability requires qualified review before production launch.
- **REQ-040** (Ubiquitous): The system shall pass the reproducible performance, accessibility, and compatibility checks in PLAN.md. Acceptance: default-theme representative routes meet the documented lab budgets; field CWV remains unverified until sufficient real traffic exists.
- **REQ-041** (Ubiquitous): The project shall provide pinned workspace tooling, reproducible local setup, automated unit/integration/browser checks, and reproducible web-app and jobs builds. Acceptance: a clean checkout can seed two synthetic tenants and execute all required checks without production access.
- **REQ-042** (Ubiquitous): The platform shall provide structured redacted telemetry, migration and release procedures, and tested backup/restore and rollback plans. Acceptance: an isolated restore recovers a seeded tenant, a failed provider job is retryable, and a prior compatible application build can serve the current schema.
- **REQ-043** (Event-driven; amended 2026-11-18): When an owner of a paid website connects a custom domain, the system shall support a hostname such as `www.example.com`, a subdomain such as `catalog.example.com`, and an apex such as `example.com` where verified provider/DNS capabilities permit it; show exact DNS instructions, ownership and TLS progress, safe retry, primary-domain selection, alias redirect and removal within REQ-065 limits (one domain name per website; www is the default primary; owner removal only before activation, afterwards operator-only). Trial websites cannot connect a custom domain (DEC-035 C4). Acceptance: the complete connect-to-active-to-remove journey passes for two tenants, an unverified domain cannot serve content, a domain cannot be claimed twice, a trial website is refused, and removal restores the verified platform subdomain before disabling the custom hostname. Apex limitations must be visible before setup rather than reported as success. The connect flow (DEC-040, PLAN section 3.2) picks one of three modes from the owner's answer: new domain, live-site migration with zero downtime (hostname ownership and certificate are pre-validated while the old site keeps serving, and the CNAME instruction unlocks only when both `status` and `ssl.status` are `active`), or a domain already on Cloudflare (O2O). Acceptance additions: in the migration mode the platform never shows the CNAME step before the certificate is active; a conflicting `_acme-challenge` TXT is detected and named; an active hostname whose CNAME or delegation record disappears triggers an owner notice before certificate expiry.

Superadmin means the platform operator, a separate platform-level authority rather than a tenant owner role. The existing no-impersonation constraint remains. Infrastructure secrets and unrestricted customer data browsing are not admin features.

Acceptance: audit records cannot be changed through UI/API, all privileged mutations record actor/reason/target/outcome, operator revocation affects subsequent requests, tenant users cannot query platform summaries, and no secrets or lead bodies appear in platform logs/views.

## 16. Value Additions Approved 2026-11-14 (DEC-026, DEC-027, ADR-0008)

Context: market desk research (BUILD-LOG 2026-11-14) found that SME owners are blocked mainly by time and technical skill, that only 1.36% of online-selling Indonesian businesses use a website while 94.76% sell through messaging (BPS Statistik E-Commerce 2024), and that Google ended Business Profile websites in 2024.
